## Nightmare — currently 0-3 ❌

**This is a punishing failure, not a skill gap.** 📊

- All his fast moves (i14–22) are **-16 to -22**
- All his safe moves are **i26+**
- Fastest strike is **i14** — slower than Groh, Geralt, Zasalamel, Cassandra
- Only i12 option is a **command throw** → crouchable

### Plan
1. **Block and punish.** Nearly everything fast he does is launch-punishable.
2. **Break his throws.** His speed threat is the grab, not the button.
3. ❌ **Do NOT panic-RE.** He's full of `[BA]` which goes straight through RE.
4. ✅ PXS entry is free here.
5. Armor: absorbs 1 hit then auto-GIs. **Doesn't work vs. lows or 22+ damage moves.**
   ⚠️ 2026-10-24 correction 📚: true `L` only — `SL` (Ivy `2A`) **is** absorbed. Throws and BA/UA pass; **attack throws can be absorbed**.
   ✅ 2026-11-20 lab (Ivy): lows, grabs (incl. `2A+G`/`3A+G`) and ≥22-dmg hits (`3B`) beat Soul Wave. Crouch → `FC 1B` punishes `6AA`/`6K`. **`6BB` can't be block-punished (pushback) → sidestep it.**
   → **Full counter-matchup: [`Nightmare/counter-matchup.md`](Nightmare/counter-matchup.md)** · data: `Nightmare/framedata.md`

## Geralt — "tortuous", heavy punishment

- i12 `A`/`K` highs, i12 `2A`.
- `BB(B)` is **-2 with KND** — his best button.
- Most of his kit is -8 to -10. **Fewer free punishes than the others.**

### What worked ✅
Sidestep his REs · `3B` → `6K(A)` (now upgraded) · `AA` as interrupt · `2A`/`2K` to open guard

### Fixes
1. `3B` → **`3B+KK`** = 73 dmg. Fewer launches needed.
2. **Stop whiffing `3B`/`22B` in neutral** — that's where the 40% punishes come from.
   Poke with `66K` (i14) and `6A` (i14) instead.
3. RE whiff-punish: `3B` is i18 = slow. Start with `66K` (i14), upgrade later.
