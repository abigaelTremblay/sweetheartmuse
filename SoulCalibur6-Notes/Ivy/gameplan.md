# Ivy — Secondary

188 moves, 4 stances, 19 fast (i≤14), 18 fast-and-safe.

**Why picked:** maximal contrast to Xianghua (anti-burnout), answers to the Nightmare/Zasalamel range problems.

## Core identity ✅ (revised 2026-10-15)

**Short-range grappler with options at all ranges.** Close range first; long range is a neutral complement.

Why the change (my lab, 2026-10-15):
- Long-range tools need **pinpoint spacing**. All of them can be sidestepped, and they hit one distance the opponent walks out of (backing off or rushing in).
- `9B+K`, `8B+K > 6B8` and `3A+G` all end with the opponent **nose-to-nose** with me.
- Not a Seong Mi-na/Zasalamel-style zoner that lives at perfect spacing.

> ~~"True zoner — push them out, force a re-approach into my long-range tools" (session 1, "thesis confirmed")~~ → superseded.

### ⚠️ The gap: payoff
vs Astaroth/Hilde (friend, 2026-10-15): my spacing, lows and jabs "tickle"; they take **¼ HP per opening**.
→ I need **damaging payoffs off short-range punishes** (see [Payoff tree](#payoff-tree)).
✅ 2026-11-20: **still no solid punish game.** I've done better with spacing and grabs. The Nightmare lab ([counter-matchup](../Nightmare/counter-matchup.md)) is the current punish practice.

## Corrections log

### ✅ `8B+K` / `9B+K` — Break Attack launcher, not a grab
📊 +10 on block, M,M, i28, 26 dmg, KND, `[BA]`, tech-crouch frames 9–16.
✅ 2026-10-15: `8B+K` **launches vertically** at distance; guardable like `9B+K`. The pull-in is the **`6B8` follow-up** catching them midair (📊 `Midair Opponent 6b8` = 41 dmg throw).
> The SE pull-in grab is `SE 8B+K` Redemption (SE only, -20 on block).

### ✅ `AA` vs `AK` — situational (revised 2026-10-15)
> ~~"`AK` strictly beats `AA`. Never use `AA`."~~ (session 1) — **wrong**, it ignored `AA`'s `[LH]`.

- **Punish / poke** → `AK`: 2nd hit **MID**, -8 vs -12, +6 vs +2.
- **They keep sidestepping** → `AA`: ✅ 2nd hit = **LH vs sidestep**, longer reach than its 1st hit (less than `66[A]`).
- ✅ `AA` + `66[A]` together = anti-step coverage at two ranges.
- `AK` out of guard ✅: quick chip, **no outright advantage** like Xianghua's `k6` (📊 -8 blk; the +6 is on hit).

### ✅ `22(A)`/`88(A)` Wolf Lash — LH condition found (2026-10-15)
LH only on the **2nd, farther hit**, and only if they are **8-way stepping/running**.
📊 HIGH, i26, -12 blk, 0 hit. Otherwise still weak → `66A`/`4A`.
📚 `22_88A` LH **from their back** > `CS` = 85.

### ⚠️ `66(A)` Sparking Blade is **-4 on HIT**
📊 -12 on block. Pushback untested (Q7). Value = guard damage (Burst 10) + `66[A]` LH (2nd hit, long range).

### ✅ Low grabs `2A+G`/`3A+G` — DO catch crouchers (2026-10-15)
> ~~"✅ Low throws don't catch crouchers — `2A+G` whiffs entirely on a crouching opponent, immune."~~ (session 1) — **superseded.**

- They're a **low attack leading into an animation**, not a true grab (📊 L, `[TH]`, -14 on block).
- Crouching, not blocking → **caught**. Crouch **block** → **blocked**.
- So: vs **standing** guard. Not a crouch-beater against someone blocking low.

### ✅ `3A+G` is +16 on hit
Drags them in + free follow-up. 📚 `3A+G > 66BB` = 59 → +14 ranged mixup.
