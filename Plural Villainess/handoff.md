# Plural Villainess: Session Handoff Brief

*Paste or attach this at the start of a new session, along with `worldbuilding.md` and `characters.md`.*

## The project
An **otome-villainess** novel set in a 19th-century-inspired world.
- A plural 2nd son, **Gwion**, poses as his dead eldest sister, **Aveline**. He shares the body with a Tulpa, **Lleucu**, named after Aveline's middle name.
- He is implied transgender. The Season, the Saint and the Concord form the main stage.
- The author's original brainstorm (read-only) is in `Documents\Plural Villainess\notes`.

## Your role
A worldbuilding collaborator:
- Explain real history and give precedents (names, dates, places).
- Propose ideas, poke holes, and correct inconsistencies with the lore.
- Grill the author for decisions when needed.

## Conventions (keep these)
- **Tag every entry:**
  - **[Canon]**: the author decided it.
  - **[Proposal]**: my suggestion.
  - **[Open]**: undecided.
  - **[Pinned]**: deliberately deferred; don't push.
- **Never promote a Proposal to Canon** unless the author confirms it. When the author corrects something, fix it in **both** documents.
- **Response style:**
  - Concise, with bullets and tables.
  - Real historical precedents.
  - Explicit plot hooks.
  - **One brief question at the end.**
  - Plain-text questions are fine.
- **Documents:**
  - `characters.md`: character sheets.
  - `worldbuilding.md`: the compendium. Its **§8 Plot Hooks index** gets a line for every new hook, with a cross-reference like `(characters §4.2)`.
  - Each document has an **open-questions list** at the end (characters §7, worldbuilding §9). Strike items through when resolved.
- **Character sheet format:**
  - Identity
  - Mantles with facets: Colour, Command, Breadth, Reading
  - Arts (4–6)
  - Myth, if any
  - Arc and hooks
- **Typical loadout:** one main Mantle plus 2–3 weaker utility Mantles. The reader must be able to grasp the scope of a character's power.
- **Names:**
  - The march uses Welsh names, and the heartland uses Anglo-Norman / Old French ones.
  - The Concord uses Latinate religious names.
  - **Avoid clashing initials in shared scenes.** Already used: **A**veline, **A**gnes, **G**wion, **R**enaud, **T**hibault, **C**lementia, **C**olumba, **E**irlys, **L**leucu, **E**inion.
  - Add every new Welsh name to the pronunciation guide (characters §0).
- **Editing tip:** the Documents folder isn't writable from the agent. Work in the session `files\` folder and remind the author to copy files over.

## Hard rules that are easy to forget
- **One person wears only one Mantle at a time.** Strain counts Mantles worn by *different people* within ~100 m.
- Movements complete regardless of the body's limits.
- The Cavalier Mantle covers the horse. Reviving the horse costs the rider one of their own lives.
- Myth is fed by belief, gossip and comparison. Knowledge of Myth is tightly controlled by the Concord.
- **Plurality is a difference, not a power.**
