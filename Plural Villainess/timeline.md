# Plural Villainess: Timeline

*Chronological spine of the story: the stations of canon and the key moments. Not every hook belongs here; the full hook list lives in worldbuilding §8.*

## 0. How to read this page
- **Tags:** [Canon], [Proposal], [Open], [Pinned], as in the other documents. A [Proposal] here is often only a **placement** of a canon event (where it falls in the order), not new content.
- **Years:** "Y" counts years from the first bastard's birth, as in the original `notes`. **Y18 = Gwion is 13 and the book opens. Y21 = the Season** (Gwion 16, posing as 18).
- **Aveline's agency column:**
  - ✅ **Active:** Aveline (Gwion, Lleucu or both) makes a meaningful decision that changes what happens.
  - ⚠️ **Passive:** things happen *to* her, or someone else makes the decision. **To fix later.**
  - ◐ **Partial:** she reacts well, but the scene is triggered or resolved by someone else.
  - — : not her scene.
- **Endgame:** a triad of **Aveline, Agnes/Nell and Renaud.** Every arc track in §3 is checked against it.
- **Gaps** are marked **[Gap]**. They show where the story still needs fleshing out.

## 4. Conflicts to discuss
1. **C1. Thibault's climax vs the triad.** His route climax is the "true exchange, no reserve" in the Lists, *where the Green Blade shows herself.* But he isn't in the endgame, and the Green Blade turn is Aveline's own milestone. **Should her biggest Martial moment belong to a man she doesn't end up with?** Options: split them (the exchange is his closure; the Green Blade comes later, for her own reasons), or make the exchange the moment she lays the ghost to rest in front of him.
2. **C2. Renaud's growth undercuts her body.** His belief fuels her transition and fertility **because the public thinks he's good.** His climax (breaking the Good Prince script) risks a fallen-prince story that weakens that belief, maybe right before the wedding. Either order the beats (wedding first, defiance after), or make the crowd's new story ("a good man") strong enough to carry it.
3. **C3. Whose name is "Nell"?** Gwion's early use of "Nell" backfires. Columba treasures it as her only possession. The way-out proposal uses a renaming to slip the oath. If Agnes takes back "Nell," does it connect her to Columba's open channel (the risk that the god pours through both)? Options: she reclaims "Nell" and *shares* it with Columba (a hopeful link), or she chooses a name nobody gave her.
4. **C4. The public villainess vs a respectable triad.** All season the press writes Aveline as jealous of the Saint. The triad's cover (chaplain or companion) needs the public to believe they're friends. **There's no beat yet where the public story turns.** Aveline is the Role-craft expert, so the turn should be **her** campaign, maybe through Eirlys's column.
5. **C5. When is the wedding?** "Audition to keep the contract" plus the banns at station 13 imply a wedding near the Season's end. But Agnes's exit, Clementia's fall and the triad seem to need the autumn or later. Options: the wedding as the climax, the wedding as the epilogue, or a delay (the Duchess demands a second Season).
6. **C6. My own proposal conflicts with Renaud's refusal.** The "locks and keys" table says "a royal-adjacent marriage is the one exit the public accepts." Renaud refuses to marry Agnes, and Aveline marries him. **I'd replace this** with "a place in a royal household" (chaplain to Renaud's house), which matches route 2 of the triad road. Confirm.
7. **C7. Renaud's age (minor).** "~19 at the Season" and "~10 at the formalisation" don't fit the years: formalisation at Y10 and the Season at Y21 put him at **8** then. Either he's 21 at the Season, or he was 8.
8. **C8. Agnes's "Myth transfer" must come before intimacy.** It isn't a contradiction but an ordering rule: a lover who sees a hidden eye makes it persist, and her purity holds up her stability. So **"I hold the god" (X3) has to come before the triad becomes physical (R3).**
